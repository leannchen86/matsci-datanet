ImageNet question:
- ImageNet "moment" was a sequence: 2009 dataset (3.2M images, 5,247 categories) -> ILSVRC 2010-2017 with hidden test labels, classification data fixed from 2012 -> AlexNet 2012 15.3% vs 26.2% top-5 error (two GTX 580s) -> 24 teams in 2013 vs 21 in prior three years combined -> pretrain/fine-tune "de facto standard" by 2016 (Huh et al.). [SOURCE]
- Noisy labels, stable rankings: 6% of validation labels wrong; Recht et al. new test set cut accuracy 11-14% but rankings held. Inference: consistent protocol mattered more than perfect labels. [SOURCE + OPINION]
- Scale is not the missing piece: OQMD 1,407,395 materials; OMat24 118M structures; MPtrj 1,580,395 configs; CSD >1.4M; MEAD 1.5M samples; HTEM 141,574 entries; NOMAD 2023 >12M simulations vs ~50k experiment/synthesis entries. Labels mix DFT flavours (MP: GGA/GGA+U/r2SCAN; OC20: RPBE no spin). [SOURCE]
- Label checking: ImageNet crowd votes 99.7% precision on 80 sampled categories; for DFT the calculation is the label; MP launch paper: "some of the data will be incorrect". Starrydata2 >194,000 hand-digitized curves. Multi-lab skutterudite comparison: 19% relative standard uncertainty on ZT. [SOURCE]
- Bulk access, not license type, is the blocker: OQMD/OMat24 CC BY, MPtrj MIT, GNoME non-commercial, HTEM/MEAD CC BY 4.0; ICSD API tied to one named project; MPDS API from EUR 9,500/yr academic; PoLyInfo bans bulk download. ImageNet itself was non-commercial-only. [SOURCE + OPINION]
- Computed side partway there: MACE-MP-0 fine-tunes with ~100 configs; GemNet-OC OC20->OC22 energy error 0.374 -> 0.239 eV; Matbench Discovery (day of answer) 43 of 53 eligible models, top F1 0.931, best MPtrj-only 0.863, top four add OMat24+sAlex. Limits: all 0 K DFT; ignores entropy/disorder/dynamic stability; Open Catalyst 2021 winner 0.5474 eV vs ~0.2 eV practical. [SOURCE] — bulk-stability screening is near a moment; synthesizability/performance is not [OPINION].
- Experimental validation disputed: GNoME 736 matches to ICSD ("lower bound"); Cheetham & Seshadri found scant novelty/credibility/usefulness; A-Lab Jan 2026 correction 41 of 58 -> 36 of 57 targets, "novel" removed from title; Leeman et al.: ~two-thirds of claimed successes likely known disordered versions. Separate count: 36 of 40 claimed successes confirmed, 4 unsettled from XRD alone, Zn2Cr3FeO8 dropped (in training data). [SOURCE]
- Problems ImageNet never had: specimen identity; biased literature (17% of amines in 79% of hydrothermal compounds; smaller randomized set beat larger human-chosen sets, Jia 2019; Sun & David 2025 on text-mined recipes); missing failures (30% of A-Lab's 353 recipes produced targets; MEAD v1 omitted failed measurements; ~10% of HTEM entries in papers). [SOURCE]
- Better analog = PDB + CASP: PDB 1971 with 7 structures; 1989 IUCr deposition policy (embargo 1 yr coordinates / 4 yr structure factors); journals + NIGMS required accession codes early 1990s; raw structure factors from 2008; validation reports by 2017; CASP since 1994; AlphaFold2 (2020) GDT_TS > 90 on ~two-thirds of targets, archive <150,000 structures until 2019 (plus 2.2B BFD sequences, ~350,000 self-predicted structures). [SOURCE]; "defining specimens, deposition rules and a blind test matter more than volume" [OPINION]. Materials precedent: CSD + CCDC blind tests (molecular crystals only; one leak incident).

## 5. Problems / gaps identified (gap — evidence — who it hurts — [STATUS])
- No agreed definition of "the same specimen" / no persistent sample id — PDLLA 3 Mw values; Ghiringhelli et al. — anyone merging experimental records. [OWN-EXPERIMENT + SOURCE]
- Missing uncertainty and silently flattened censored values — 6 of 9; ">300" -> 300 — model trainers and evaluators. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- Benchmarks strip provenance and average away spread — Matbench quartz, matbench_steels 842 -> 312 — benchmark users. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- Undefined release fields / undisclosed splits — GNoME `Is Train`; Periodic releases no split — reproducibility. [OWN-EXPERIMENT + SOURCE]
- Almost no experimental benchmark with hidden labels or blind tests — matbench_steels 312 rows; CCDC only molecular crystals. [SOURCE]
- Between-lab spread unmeasured for most properties — 19% on ZT for one skutterudite. [SOURCE]
- Failures and unbiased sampling missing from literature-derived data. [SOURCE]
- Reference-structure licensing (ICSD/ICDD/CSD) restricts AI/identification use; exclusivity deals emerging — hurts any public XRD benchmark or tool. [SOURCE + OPINION]
- LLM-judge evaluation with judges that also compete, no blinding, no known-answer cases, no error bars, no per-sample results (Periodic). Cited risks: Gao 2022, Zhao 2025, Panickssery 2024, MT-Bench self-preference. [SOURCE + OPINION]
- Atlas limits: one record per case, cannot say how common any gap is; defines no task/split/metric; not covered: tomography, in situ, MD, CALPHAD, battery cycling, sintered ceramics, proprietary DBs. [OWN-EXPERIMENT, stated limit]

## 10. Jargon that matters for the big picture
- Powder XRD — X-ray fingerprint of a powder; "phase identification" = multi-label classification of which crystal structures are present, done by fitting candidate reference structures.
- DFT / PBE — quantum simulation used as the label generator; like training on a simulator with systematic bias (PBE quartz cell too large).
- 0 K label — computed with no temperature/entropy/disorder; sim-to-real gap.
- Specimen identity — what counts as "the same example"; composition alone is not a key.
- Provenance — lineage from label back to raw source (dataset lineage).
- Round robin — same samples measured by several labs; inter-annotator agreement for instruments.
- Blind test (CASP, CCDC) — test targets not yet public; true held-out set.
- Deposition policy — journal/funder rule forcing data upload; the incentive layer.
- ZT — thermoelectric figure of merit (the target variable for thermoelectrics).
- ICSD / ICDD PDF / CSD — commercial reference-structure databases with AI-restrictive licences; COD (CC0) and MP (CC BY) are the open ones.
- opXRD / RRUFF / Dara / XRDBench — public XRD pattern sets and benchmarks.
- LLM-as-judge — model grades model; verdicts are ratings, not ground truth.
- Matbench / Matbench Discovery — existing ML leaderboards (computed labels mostly).
- A-Lab / autonomous lab — robot synthesis loop; XRD is its success gate.
- OPTIMADE / NeXus / CIF — existing interchange standards to build a schema on.

## 12. Smart or non-obvious ideas worth not losing
- Classify every data gap by fix type (documentation / new measurement / new test / new calculation): it separates what a data person can do alone from what needs a lab.
- The scarce asset is task-specific supervision (hard samples + synthesis context + calibrated judge + clean chemical-system split), not raw records; a public effort must build it deliberately.
- A public XRD benchmark with known-answer cases (weighed mixtures, round-robin samples), per-sample results, error bars and a stated counting rule would be strictly more checkable than Periodic's private one; the pieces (Dara, opXRD, XRDBench, Dartsi et al.) already exist under open licences.
- Simple binomial noise arithmetic on small test sets (n=134 -> +/-4.3 pts) is enough to deflate headline model comparisons.
- Weather-reanalysis analogy: DFT databases are like a reanalysis with no observations mixed in; the missing ingredient is measured data assimilated into the labels.
