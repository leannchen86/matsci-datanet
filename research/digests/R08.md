## 4. Findings (each: claim — numbers/evidence — [STATUS] — "so what")

- Unwritten rule recovered: a cell is partial when something usable exists but something a reuser needs is missing/blank, exists only on the web page, or is findable only by text/structure matching — [OPINION].
- RRUFF R040031 (one natural quartz crystal, King County WA): 7 of 8 layers partial (Composition present). Firm: Structure (cell a = 4.9134(2) A measured, but atom positions copied from a 1980 study of another crystal; no temperature/handedness), Method (instrument missing for 5 of 13 Raman spectra; microprobe voltage/current missing), Provenance (no COD/AMCSD/ICSD/MP links, no license). Borderline: Processing, Signal (29 spectra/patterns; no axis names/units; "RAW" files already processed), Uncertainty (esds on cell; spread over 20 microprobe points; nothing on any spectrum), Supported tasks — [OWN-EXPERIMENT: read stored files].
- Standards that back calls: JCAMP-DX ##XUNITS/##YUNITS (RRUFF omits); CIF fields for position uncertainty, T, P, Flack parameter; GUM divides by n-1 while RRUFF divides by n; FAIR asks for links + license; Porto notation for Raman geometry; no standard for Processing / Supported tasks — [SOURCE].
- DFT vs experiment is "same material, different definitions": DFT = ideal pure crystal, atoms still, 0 K, one approximate functional; experiment = real specimen (RRUFF quartz has ~265 ppm Al), often 298 K — [SOURCE/OPINION].
- Quartz (mp-7000 vs experiment): cell volume PBE 120.34 A^3 (+6.3%), r2SCAN 113.63 (+0.37%) vs 113.21 A^3 at 298 K (COD 2300370); vs experiment extrapolated to 0 K: r2SCAN +1.2%, PBE +7.2% (r2SCAN's room-T match is partly error cancellation). Formation energy: GGA+correction -3.257, r2SCAN -3.038, mixed -3.273 vs -3.147 eV/atom NIST-JANAF (offsets -0.11 / +0.11 / -0.13). Band gap 5.72 eV GGA vs 8.9 optical / 9.65 energy-loss (36-41% low). Stability: GGA puts quartz 10.6 meV/atom ABOVE a cristobalite-type SiO2 (wrong order; experiment: cristobalite ~8.7 meV/atom above quartz); r2SCAN and mixed get it right — [OWN-EXPERIMENT on stored files + SOURCE].
- GGA energy is close only due to a fitted correction: MP adds -0.687 eV per O atom fitted to experiment; without it GGA is ~0.35 eV/atom off (Wang 2021) — [SOURCE] — corrected DFT is not independent of experiment.
- Experimental spread: quartz cells differ up to 0.19% between studies; band gaps 8.6-9.65 eV; two main thermochemistry tables agree within ~0.5 meV/atom, so the 0.11 eV/atom offsets are real — [SOURCE].
- Combining works when source-labelled: Chen 2021 adding PBE data cut experimental band-gap error ~22%; Matbench keeps expt_gap and mp_gap separate; PBE quartz cell shifts strongest XRD peak ~0.56 deg (correct cells before phase ID); Raman peaks: 11 cm^-1 average error PBE0 vs 34 cm^-1 PBE — [SOURCE].
- Jha 2019 (corrected 2020; duplicates removed 1,963 -> 1,643): experimental formation-energy error 0.0715 (OQMD-pretrained) vs 0.1325 eV/atom (experiment-only). At ~148 experimental compounds: 0.106 vs 0.436; at ~1,479: 0.071 vs 0.133. Pretrained with ~148 beats scratch with all ~1,479 — [SOURCE]. Earlier "0.06 vs 0.13-0.15" were pre-correction numbers — [REFUTED -> corrected].
- Jha 2019 did NOT vary DFT size for transfer (always all of OQMD). DFT-only Table 3: 11k/24k/341k -> 0.19/0.16/0.14 (confounded: databases differ in more than size). Jha 2022: JARVIS 20k / MP 102k / OQMD 353k -> 0.087/0.078/0.064; without fine-tuning order breaks 0.147/0.097/0.120 — [SOURCE].
- Nobody has published the clean DFT-size curve for formation energy. Closest: Chen 2021 band gaps (Supp. Table 6, joint training), error 0 -> 41k DFT: 100 expt pts 0.87->0.56 eV (-0.13 eV per 10x DFT; 41k DFT worth ~14x expt data); 200: 0.80->0.53 (-0.08; ~9x); 500: 0.71->0.51 (-0.08; ~4.5x); 1,000: 0.60->0.47 (-0.06; ~4x model-based); 2,430: 0.50->0.40 (-0.04; ~4x very uncertain). Run-to-run spread +/-0.02-0.13 eV. Total gain 0.31 eV at 100 vs 0.10 eV at 2,430 — [SOURCE table + OWN-EXPERIMENT fits, recomputed twice independently].
- Diminishing returns: at 41k another 1,000 DFT points buys 0.0002-0.001 eV; at 410k 11-31x less. Exchange rate: ~29 DFT pts per experimental pt at 100 expt (range 10-52); ~6 at 2,430 (range 0.3-37, extrapolates scratch curve; reflects flattening scratch learning, not DFT becoming more useful) — [OWN-EXPERIMENT fit; earlier unqualified "only ~6" REFUTED/softened].
- Other curves: Minami 2025 polymers error = C + D*N^-alpha with floor C = "transfer gap"; 10x more sim removes ~1.4-7.8% of error; ~221 sims per experiment point for density at ~71k sims/~600 expts (local rate only). Lee & Asahi 2021: pretrain 0/10k/54k/113k DFT formation energies -> 500 DFT band gaps 0.978/0.866/0.826/0.815 eV (last step inside ~0.09 spread). Devi 2024 non-monotonic (10k worse than 5k or 50k; 100k best). ElemNet on DFT itself ~0.35 -> 0.05 eV/atom from 464 to 231k compounds — transfer to experiment is much flatter — [SOURCE].
- Floors: calorimetry uncertainty ~0.023 eV/atom; compilations differ ~0.030; two experimental databases disagree by 0.082 on 75 intermetallics; DFT misses experiment by ~0.08-0.10 eV/atom; formula-only models cannot distinguish polymorphs; if floor > target, no DFT amount helps (Mikami). Which noise level applies to Jha's 0.07 is unknown (test set mostly oxides/nitrides/hydrides/halides, no error bars) — [SOURCE; earlier "Jha already at floor" REFUTED].
- Extrapolation unreliable: Chen spans only 1.6 decades (1k-41k); at 2,430 expt, three shapes predict 0.37-0.41 eV at 410k (band 0.26-0.47); predicted 41k->410k gain 0.009-0.040 eV vs ~0.08 eV detection limit with ~270 test compounds even with six repeats; 20k->41k at 2,430 not significant (p = 0.15); in simulations, backtest success predicting 41k from <=20k had ~zero correlation with error at 100x; any shape off by 0.02-0.18 eV at 100x; with ~2k test compounds differences below ~0.002-0.007 eV/atom invisible (test-set sampling noise; low end assumes paired scoring) — [OWN-EXPERIMENT fits/simulations]. The earlier "don't extrapolate past ~10x" rule was RETRACTED: even 10x gives a band wider than the whole measured gain — [REFUTED].
- Raw DFT count is the wrong x-axis: only ~13-55% of big DFT databases is informative; a well-chosen 20% matches a random 50-90% (Li 2023); ~250k of OQMD's ~300k entries are prototype variations, not measured compounds; a model at 0.022 eV/atom on its own split scored 0.297 on newer alloys — [SOURCE].

## 10. Jargon that matters for the big picture
- DFT — quantum-mechanical simulation of an ideal crystal at 0 K; think synthetic data from a biased simulator.
- Functional (PBE/GGA, r2SCAN, PBE0) — the approximation inside DFT; different functionals = different simulators with different systematic bias.
- Formation energy (eV/atom) — energy change when a compound forms from its elements; the standard stability label.
- Band gap (eV) — energy to free an electron to conduct.
- Convex hull / above hull — DFT ranking of stability; not proof a material exists.
- Fitted correction — post-hoc offset on DFT energies tuned to experiment (label leakage risk).
- Transfer gap / floor (C) — irreducible error in C + D*N^-alpha.
- Exchange rate — how many DFT points equal one experimental point.
- Polymorph — same formula, different crystal structure; formula-only models cannot separate them.
- Space group #152 vs #154 — mirror-image quartz labels; break naive joins.
- StructureMatcher — pymatgen structure dedupe tool that rescales volume.
- esd — estimated standard deviation on a crystallographic value.
- Flack parameter — single-crystal measure of handedness.
- OQMD / Materials Project / JARVIS — the big DFT databases (pretraining corpora).
- Ossification — pretraining hurting when fine-tune data are plentiful (Hernandez 2021).
