**C13 characterization primer** [SOURCE-level textbook facts]
- XRD scatters off electrons (signal ∝ Z): Li (Z=3) nearly invisible beside Mn (Z=25); Mn/Fe/Co/Ni (Z=25-28) look alike. Neutron scattering lengths (fm): Li −1.9, Mn −3.7, Co +2.5, Ni +10.3, O +5.8. Powder XRD is blind to amorphous material, Li/H, phases below ~1-5%.
- NPD is complementary not superior: needs reactor/spallation source (few dozen worldwide), proposal + months wait, grams and hours vs milligrams and minutes.
- Access tiers: in-house routine (XRD, SEM-EDS, ICP-OES, Raman, FTIR, TGA/DSC); shared specialist (XPS, TEM, NMR, single-crystal XRD); national facility (synchrotron, neutrons). Typical sequence: XRD -> ICP -> SEM, escalate only on ambiguity.

## 10. Jargon that matters for the big picture
- XRD (powder) — 1D fingerprint of which crystals are in a sample; like a spectrogram you must decompose into known sources.
- Phase identification — naming the crystalline compounds in a mixture; open-set multi-label classification.
- Rietveld refinement — least-squares fit of a simulated pattern to data; contains the forward model; analysis-by-synthesis.
- Forward model vs inverse problem — structure->pattern is cheap and exact; pattern->structure is many-to-one.
- MLIP — ML interatomic potential; fast learned surrogate for DFT energies/forces.
- DFT — quantum-mechanical simulation; source of most "ML materials datasets" labels (MPtrj, Matbench).
- Energy above hull — how unstable a compound is vs competitors; missing makeability filter.
- κ (thermal conductivity), ε₀ (dielectric constant) — Discovered Materials' target properties.
- BEOL — chip back-end processing, ≤~400 °C; caps what films can be made.
- PVD / co-sputtering / CVD / ALD — thin-film deposition methods.
- Reward hacking — agent games the checker (supercell duplicates, fabricated κ; adding spurious phases to lower residual).
- Rubric-distilled proxy expert / LLM judge — expert opinions turned into an LLM grader; a learned reward model never checked against ground truth.
- Neutron powder diffraction — sees nuclei not electrons; finds Li and separates Mn/Fe/Co/Ni; facility-only.
- Sim-to-real gap / domain randomization — simulated patterns are too clean; randomize the messy parts.
- TDTR / 3ω — thin-film thermal-conductivity measurement methods.

## 12. Smart or non-obvious ideas worth not losing
- Public "results" of an AI-materials startup can be audited from its own website data exports: provenance counts (504 mlip / 22 mlip-verified / 0 dft) showed zero measured data behind the claims.
- In XRD, goodness-of-fit is a falsifier, not a verifier; any reward built on residuals is hackable — which is why the field lacks a cheap automatic grader and why benchmarks with expert-adjudicated, ambiguity-aware labels are scarce.
- Spend learned capacity on the sim-to-real residual and on calibrated probabilities over phase sets, not on re-learning physics that is closed-form and sub-millisecond.
- Harness/tools + context gave 3.8x; model post-training gave a statistical tie with a frontier model — tooling and data access, not model size, is where a small team can matter.
- Both startups define "good" via LLM judges distilled from experts; an outcome-calibrated evaluation (judge verdict vs what the lab actually got) is an unfilled, externally buildable niche.
