# R03 — "Toward a Materials ImageNet" (conversation report, Sept 14–15 2026, three rounds)

## 1. What this slice is (2-3 plain sentences)
A published report summarizing one three-round conversation: Round 1 built a data "atlas" (Materials Record Wall: 9 real records from 6 material families, showing exactly where data reuse breaks); Round 2 asked whether that is an "ImageNet moment" path (answer: right direction, not sufficient; Protein Data Bank + CASP blind test is the better analogy); Round 3 compared it all to Periodic Labs' Sept 15 2026 posts (Neon model, FrontierXRD benchmark). It ends with two action tracks (A: shared XRD evaluation data for autonomous labs, 7 ranked items; B: a blind benchmark for one property family, thermoelectrics as candidate), open decisions, forecasts and explicit unknowns. The report says it "adds no new research" beyond the three chat answers; every claim is labelled Fact / Inference / Forecast / Synthesis.

## 2. What the user asked / wanted
- Goal in their words: "the imagenet moment for materials science has not happened yet, and i'm working towards making that happen" (Round 2).
- Purpose: "find gaps or opportunities of what kind of data is needed for this type of workflow and we can think of helping to curate and open-source it to help the community" (Round 3).
- Round 1: understand materials data well enough to find meaningful reuse gaps; tie Anubhav Jain's three concerns (scattered data, missing metadata, no defined problems/metrics) and Ghiringhelli et al. 2023 to real records.
- Round 2: is this direction heading toward / sufficient for an ImageNet moment?
- Round 3: how does "our data problem" relate to Periodic's work — resolved, still relevant, unrelated? Which gaps remain, what milestones are possible?
- Asked for nothing to be made up to fill space (hence labels and an "unknowns" section).

## 4. Findings
- Atlas layer audit — across 9 cases, uncertainty absent in 6 of 9; provenance only partial in 9 of 9 — [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — reuse is limited by identity/definitions/context more than by data volume [OPINION].
- Cross-database identity break — same quartz is space group P3₁21 (#152) in Materials Project (mp-7000) but P3₂21 (#154) in COD and RRUFF; RRUFF's value is inherited from literature (powder XRD cannot tell the mirror forms apart); RRUFF files carry no ICSD/MP id — [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — no join key between databases; fix is documentation.
- Cross-file identity break — one PDLLA polymer lot has three molecular weights: 15000 (file name), 12273 (vendor GPC), 10131 (SEC vs PMMA standards); files share no sample id — [OWN-EXPERIMENT].
- Benchmark provenance — Matbench quartz label −3.2741 eV/atom matches none of MP's three current formation energies (−3.2566, −3.2728, −3.0379); rows keep only structure and e_form — [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — a benchmark label cannot be traced to its source version.
- matbench_steels — 842 records (with an undefined Temperature field) averaged into 312 composition-only rows; one group of 53 records spanning 1005.9–1605.4 MPa became 1338 — [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — averaging hides a ~600 MPa spread caused by unrecorded processing.
- GNoME undefined fields — "Is Train" True on 412,757 rows, False on 72,513, blank on 68,784, defined only as "in training set for associated machine learning models"; Density documented as Å³/atom but values match g/cm³ — [OWN-EXPERIMENT].
- Uncertainty loss — MPEA processing turned ">300" into 300 with no flag — [OWN-EXPERIMENT].
- RRUFF "RAW" XRD is not raw — max exactly 100, 7,306 of 8,501 rows exactly 0, peaks shifted ~−0.035° — [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — keep native instrument files.
- Physical-model limit — PBE quartz cell 120.3357 Å³ vs 113.211 Å³ measured at 298 K; MPtrj frames and benchmark labels inherit the PBE value — [OWN-EXPERIMENT]/[SOURCE] — needs new calculations, not documentation.
- Composites study design — salt-spray coupons exist only with 8 h post-cure; seawater coupons and all non-20 °C tests only with 12 h post-cure; 19 fatigue coupons cover one lay-up, R = 0.1, 20 °C — [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — environment effect confounded with cure schedule; needs new tests. [REFUTED]: the Round 1 chat said lay-up changes coincide with cure-schedule change; corrected — lay-up never varies; the confounded factor is test environment.
- ImageNet was a sequence, not a dataset — 2009 paper 3.2M images/5,247 categories; ILSVRC 2010–2017 with hidden test labels (~1.46M images); AlexNet 2012 15.3% vs 26.2% top-5 error; 24 teams in 2013 vs 21 in prior three years combined; pretrain+fine-tune "de facto standard" by 2016 (Huh et al.) — [SOURCE].
- ImageNet labels were noisy — audit found 6% validation labels wrong (authors estimate nearer 20%); Recht et al. new test set dropped accuracy 11–14% but rankings held — [SOURCE] — consistent protocol mattered more than perfect labels [OPINION].
- Computed side is "partway there" — OQMD 1,407,395 materials; OMat24 118M structures; MPtrj 1,580,395 configs; MACE-MP-0 fine-tunes with ~100 configs; Matbench Discovery top F1 0.931 (best MPtrj-only 0.863); but every label is 0 K DFT; 2021 Open Catalyst winner 0.5474 eV error vs ~0.2 eV practical need — [SOURCE].
- Experimental checks disputed — A-Lab Jan 2026 correction 41/58 -> 36/57 targets; Leeman et al.: ~two-thirds of A-Lab successes likely known disordered versions; Cheetham & Seshadri critiqued GNoME novelty; only 30% of A-Lab's 353 recipes made target — [SOURCE].
- Experimental data is large by count but unusable as one corpus — CSD >1.4M, MEAD 1.5M samples, HTEM 141,574 entries (only ~10% described in papers); NOMAD 2023: >12M simulations vs ~50k experiment/synthesis entries; skutterudite multi-lab comparison gave 19% relative standard uncertainty on ZT — [SOURCE].
- Literature bias — in reported hydrothermal syntheses 17% of amines appear in 79% of compounds; models on a smaller randomized set beat larger human-chosen sets (Jia et al. 2019) — [SOURCE].
- PDB + CASP analog — PDB began 1971 with 7 structures; 1989 IUCr deposition policy (release delays up to 1 yr coordinates / 4 yr structure factors); journals required accession codes by early 1990s; structure factors required from 2008; CASP since 1994; AlphaFold2 (2020) matched experiment on ~two-thirds of targets, trained on PDB to April 2018 (archive passed 150,000 only in 2019) plus 2.2B BFD sequences and ~350,000 self-predicted structures — [SOURCE] — raw volume is not the main constraint; specimen definition, deposition rules, blind test are [OPINION].
- Periodic Labs (Sept 15 2026 posts) — Neon: 1T-param model post-trained from open-weight Kimi K2.6 (midtraining + RL); FrontierXRD 134 hard lab samples, accepted fits average five phases; Neon 55.3% vs base 2.7%; held-out chemical systems set of 198 measurements; Opus 5 high effort 31.53% with Periodic harness vs 8.33% with Claude Code + COD/MP/BGMN at similar cost ($5.41 vs $4.56); judge agreement: expert–expert 77.2%, LLM ensemble–expert 74.6%, ensemble–consensus 84%; three PhD experts per pattern on thousands of calibration patterns; evaluation scored by Opus 5 + GPT-5.6-Sol ensemble; nothing released except infrastructure code — [SOURCE].
- Critique of Periodic's evidence — Neon (xhigh) leads GPT-6 Astra (max) by ~2 points with SE ~4.3 points = statistical tie; cost ≈$4.4 vs ≈$7.4 per problem but different cost bases; harness comparison is not an ablation; 31.53% and 8.33% are not whole-number fractions of 134; "scaling lab data" step ≈12->17% is ~one SE; expert ratings grade analysis quality, not phase answers — [OPINION] (arithmetic on source numbers).
- Open paired synthesis + raw XRD totals ~10³ records (lower bound): Precursor Genome 1,035 pairwise reactions, 1,351 raw scans, CC BY; A-Lab GPSS 352 samples, CC BY; text-mined recipes 80,806 but no failures; opXRD 92,552 patterns, only 2,179 labelled, free-text descriptions; Dara 20 weighed mixtures (10 binary, 10 ternary); XRDBench 100 QA + 34 end-to-end tasks with published answers — [SOURCE].
- Time to a million labels from one lab: ~27 yr at 100 exp/day; ~9–14 yr at hundreds/day; ~130 yr at A-Lab's ~21/day — [OPINION] (forecast arithmetic) — a training corpus is far off; evaluation data is the reachable piece.

## 5. Problems / gaps identified
- No agreed definition of "the same specimen/material" for experimental data — taxonomy per family (crystals: handedness; glasses: thermal history, OH; metals: processing, microstructure, impurities; polymers: chain-length distribution, tacticity, end groups, moisture history; thin films: substrate, thickness, position; composites: constituents, architecture, cure, conditioning) — hurts anyone pooling data — [OPINION] built on [OWN-EXPERIMENT].
- Handling-history fields missing from every schema checked — pdCIF PD_PREP has no atmosphere/humidity field; NeXus NXsample and NOMAD record atmosphere only as a category; none has humidity, exposure-time or storage fields — hurts XRD phase analysis (oxide vs hydroxide depends on moisture exposure) — [SOURCE].
- No hidden-label XRD or synthesis benchmark found; public XRD benchmarks publish answers and are probably in LLM pretraining data — hurts clean evaluation of LLM agents — [SOURCE]/[OPINION].
- No multi-rater label set for hard multiphase reaction products (NIST/IMMI 2021 covers one Nb-doped VO₂ film system; Precursor Genome has two scores + arbiter) — [SOURCE]. 2002 Search-Match Round Robin: 1 of 25 participants identified all 10 phases at first step — experts are not ground truth.
- No external ground truth checks the experts or the judge; weighed mixtures exist only at n = 20, 2–3 phases (Dara) — [SOURCE].
- Disorder/handedness references locked by licence — ~half of ICSD entries show disorder but ICSD API licences are per named project; ICDD bars ML derivatives; CuspAI announced exclusive AI-training rights to CSD, ICSD, Wiley data (scope unstated); MPDS API from EUR 9,500/yr; PoLyInfo bans bulk download — [SOURCE].
- Negatives without measurement floors — 3DSC encodes 3,854 non-superconductors as Tc = 0 K with no floor; Hosono's ~700 negatives give floors only in prose; SuperCon has a tcn floor field; no raw R(T)/M(T) repository found — [SOURCE].
- Private labs hold the new data — none of Periodic, Lila, Radical AI, CuspAI, DeepMind's planned UK lab has released own-experiment data (in pages read) — [SOURCE]/[OPINION].
- Atlas's own limits — one record per case so prevalence unknown; defines no task/split/metric; no deposit incentive; no costs; omits tomography, in situ, MD, CALPHAD, batteries, sintered ceramics, ICSD — [OPINION].

## 7. Decisions made and things ruled out
- Evaluation data before training corpus (PDB + CASP route) — because open paired data is ~10³ vs ~1.46M ILSVRC images, and company labs have little reason to release evaluation pieces.
- Non-commercial licence is not the blocker (ImageNet was non-commercial too); bulk access is.
- Do not build a new harness for A4; extend XRDBench.
- A7 deprioritized: Periodic's posts say nothing on property-data use.
- Keep native raw files (.xrdml, Bruker RAW); keep judge labels separate from expert labels.
- Not decided: Track A vs Track B first (both first steps are desk work, so neither blocks the other).

## 10. Jargon that matters
- XRD — X-ray fingerprint of which crystal structures are in a powder; phase analysis = multi-label classification plus mixture fractions.
- Multiphase / phase — a sample that is a mixture of several crystal structures.
- Rietveld refinement / Rwp — curve-fitting candidate structures to the pattern; Rwp is the residual (low loss can still be the wrong answer).
- Enantiomorph — mirror-image crystal; powder XRD usually cannot distinguish them (label ambiguity).
- Ordered vs disordered — databases may store an idealized version of a structure with randomly shared sites.
- Harness — tools + data + instructions given to an LLM agent.
- LLM judge — model grading answers against a rubric; needs calibration against humans.
- Sequestered split — hidden test labels (Kaggle private leaderboard).
- Weighed mixture — sample made from known amounts, giving analyst-independent ground truth.
- Round robin — same specimen measured by many labs to get between-lab noise.
- Measurement floor — lowest temperature/limit reached; a negative without it is an unbounded label.
- Handling history — air/moisture exposure, storage, time from synthesis to measurement (missing covariates).
- PDB + CASP — archive with deposition rules plus neutral blind contest; the proposed model instead of ImageNet.
- DFT / PBE — simulated 0 K labels; computed side's "ground truth".
- Krippendorff's α — chance-corrected multi-rater agreement.

## 12. Smart or non-obvious ideas worth not losing
- The community's opening shifted from "build the training corpus" to "build the shared, sequestered, multi-rater evaluation set + context schema" — small enough to exist years before an ImageNet-scale corpus, and the piece private labs will not release.
- Periodic's own list of "what decides an analysis" (precursors, temperature, atmosphere, handling history, related samples) is a ready-made target list for the missing-metadata schema.
- Keep every rater's label and keep judge labels as a separate versioned source; closed judge models get retired, so include an open-weight judge.
- Sample-size arithmetic tells you what a benchmark can resolve: ~270 items for ±5 pts agreement; 100–200 items separates harness gaps (31.5% vs 8.3%) but not 2–5 pt model gaps (~770–1,570 items); paired scoring needs fewer.
- Start narrow (ImageNet launched with 12 top-level categories; 127 vs 1000 pretraining classes cost only 2.8 mAP): one property family, one task.
