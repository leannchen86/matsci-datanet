## 4. Findings
**A. The atlas labels**
- "Partial" was assistant judgment, not a benchmark/rubric. Wall = 72 cells: present 12, partial 50, absent 8, not-applicable 2, unknown 0, not-in-public-release 0. Legend defines only absent/unknown/not applicable/not in public release; "present" and "partial" (62 of 72 cells) are undefined, and there is no roll-up rule from field states to a cell. [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — the atlas's headline grid is not reproducible by someone else.
- Unwritten rule (descriptive): partial = something usable exists for that layer but something a reuser needs is missing, blank, web-page-only, or reachable only by matching. [OPINION]
- RRUFF R040031: Composition present; 7 layers partial. Firm: Structure (cell measured on this crystal a=4.9134(2), c=5.4042(4), V=112.991(5) A^3, but atom positions copied from COD 9000775, Levien et al. 1980, a different crystal; no temperature; handedness inherited), Method (instrument missing for 5 of 13 Raman spectra; microprobe voltage/current blank; several settings only on web page), Provenance (RRUFF id in all 27 text arrays; 0 ICSD/MP ids; AMCSD only via name search; no license). Borderline: Processing, Signal, Uncertainty, Supported tasks. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- "RAW" is not raw: 29 arrays with no axis names/units (except 2 NVIR CSVs); XRD RAW scaled to max 100, 7,306 of 8,501 rows zero, shifted ~-0.035 deg; IR RAW has 638 negative values; Raman Processed = RAW minus background with endpoints forced to 0; native instrument files "No Files Uploaded". Uncertainty: esds on cell only; microprobe SD over 20 points divides by n (GUM uses n-1); nothing on arrays. [OWN-EXPERIMENT] — fine for peak positions/phase ID, not quantitative intensity work.
- Two real labeling inconsistencies: (1) exists-but-null fields are "absent" for MP (`energy_uncertainy_per_atom` null) and HTEM (`fpm_standard_deviation` null at all 44 positions) but "unknown" for RRUFF blanks; following the legend changes "Uncertainty absent in 6 of 9 cases" to 4 of 9. (2) matching-only links: absent for Matbench 127586 and RRUFF, partial for COD 2300370/9000775; same RRUFF<->9000775 link is absent at one end, partial at the other. Nine other choices defensible but undocumented (e.g., GNoME Processing absent vs MP not applicable; HTEM Signal present despite 502 raw files). One audit finding (F3, "not in public release dominates") REFUTED — it counted field labels, not wall cells. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- Standards that could anchor layers: JCAMP-DX ##XUNITS/##YUNITS (Signal), IUCr coreCIF incl. Flack parameter (Structure), GUM (Uncertainty), FAIR (Provenance), Porto notation (Raman Method); none for Processing/Supported tasks. Atlas cites JCAMP-DX/Porto only as fixes, FAIR only in its timeline, GUM never; Ghiringhelli et al. 2023 wh-questions set no thresholds. [SOURCE]

**B. DFT vs experiment (alpha-quartz, MP mp-7000)**
- Not apples/oranges: same material, different definitions, known offsets. Compare after aligning definitions; never merge into one unlabeled column. [OPINION]
- Cell volume: PBE 120.3357 A^3 (+6.29%), r2SCAN 113.62545 (+0.37%) vs experiment 113.211 at 298 K (COD 2300370, Lignie 2012, Flack -0.02(17)). Vs 0 K experiment (V~112.27): r2SCAN +1.2%, PBE +7.2%; vs static lattice (110.73): +2.6% / +8.7%. r2SCAN's room-temperature match is partly cancellation with thermal expansion (+0.66-0.84%). Experimental cells across studies spread 0.19% (112.991-113.211). Typical PBE lattice error +1.1% (44 solids, Tran 2016); framework silica is a known bad case. [OWN-EXPERIMENT recomputed + SOURCE]
- Formation energy (eV/atom): GGA+MP2020 -3.2566 (-0.110 vs expt), r2SCAN -3.0379 (+0.109), mixed -3.2728 (-0.126); NIST-JANAF -3.1468 at 298 K; CODATA agrees within ~0.5 meV/atom. Three MP values span 0.235. Matbench label -3.2741 matches none, has no id/version (predates MP2020; legacy correction gives -3.2668). MP2020 oxide correction -0.687 eV/O is fitted to experiment (222 compounds, MAE 51 meV/atom, Wang 2021); without it GGA ~0.35 eV/atom off. [OWN-EXPERIMENT + SOURCE] — "corrected DFT" is not independent of experiment.
- Band gap: MP 5.719 eV (2013 GGA task; summary file doesn't name the functional while structure/energy are r2SCAN) vs 8.9 eV optical / 9.65 eV EELS: 35.7-40.7% low; PBE averages -41.1% over 472 solids (Borlido 2019). MP r2SCAN 6.631, HSE06 7.527. [SOURCE]
- Stability: GGA puts quartz 10.58 meV/atom above mp-546794 (is_stable false); r2SCAN/mixed put it on the hull; experiment: cristobalite ~8.7 meV/atom above quartz. Polymorph gaps (~10 meV/atom) < typical formation-energy errors (51-136 meV/atom). [SOURCE/OWN]
- Hull != synthesizability both ways: 50.5% of observed phases metastable, median 15 meV/atom (Sun 2016); GNoME 2.2M below hull, 736 independently made; A-Lab found no correlation with synthesis success. [SOURCE]
- Join traps: MP lists space group #152, experiment #154 (mirror images; powder XRD cannot tell them apart) -> joining on space-group number drops RRUFF; pymatgen StructureMatcher defaults (ltol 0.2, stol 0.3, angle_tol 5, scale=True) rescale volume so a 6.3%-too-big cell still "matches"; may also match mirror images (untested). mp-7000 has 37 ICSD ids but ICSD structures are not public. Uncertainty lost in merges: top-level field null; only 0.002 eV/atom buried on the correction. [OWN-EXPERIMENT/SOURCE]
- XRD/Raman consequences: PBE quartz cell moves strongest XRD line ~0.56 deg (26.082 vs 26.642 deg); A-Lab corrects DFT lattices before simulating XRD. Computed quartz Raman peaks MAE 11 cm^-1 (PBE0) vs 34 (PBE) (Li 2024). RRUFF specimen has ~265 ppm Al; DFT cell is ideal. [SOURCE/OWN]
- Legitimate combinations: functional benchmarking; fidelity-labelled multi-fidelity ML (Chen 2021, ~22% lower MAE on experimental gaps); transfer learning; Matbench keeps expt_gap and mp_gap separate. [SOURCE]

**C. Learning curve (error vs DFT amount)**
- REFUTED earlier number: Jha 2019 "0.06 vs 0.13-0.15 eV/atom" was pre-correction. Author Correction (2020) removed duplicates 1,963 -> 1,643 compounds; corrected 10-fold CV MAE: transfer 0.0715, scratch 0.1325, OQMD-only model on experiment 0.1385, RF 0.1516. [SOURCE]
- Jha 2019 main sweep varied only experimental size: ~148 expt -> TL 0.106 / scratch 0.436; ~1,479 -> 0.071 / 0.133 (pretrained with 148 beats scratch with all). REFUTED: "never varied DFT size" — a side table has DFT-only models from 11k/24k/341k databases scoring 0.19/0.16/0.14 vs experiment, confounded by database. Jha 2022: JARVIS 20k / MP 102k / OQMD 353k -> 0.087/0.078/0.064 fine-tuned (scratch 0.327; 522 train/137 test); without fine-tuning 0.147/0.097/0.120 (not size-ordered). [SOURCE] — no clean DFT-size curve for experimental formation energy exists ("which no one has published").
- Cleanest curve: Chen 2021 SI Table 6 (experimental band-gap MAE vs PBE size 0-41k at 5 experimental sizes, 6 repeats). Assistant's fits (done twice independently): gain per 10x DFT -0.13/-0.08/-0.08/-0.06/-0.04 eV at expt N=100/200/500/1000/2430; total gain 0->41k 0.31 -> 0.10 eV (e.g., 0.87->0.56 at 100; 0.50->0.40 at 2,430); 41k PBE "worth" ~14x/9x/4.5x/4x/~4x the experimental set. Marginal +1k PBE at 41k buys 0.00016-0.0012 eV; 11-31x less at 410k. [OWN-EXPERIMENT on published table] — log-linear diminishing returns; benefit shrinks as experimental data grows. Caveat (correction): Chen is joint multi-fidelity training, not pretrain/fine-tune; their frozen-layer transfer did worse (0.47 vs 0.40 eV).
- Exchange rate 29 PBE ~ 1 experimental point at N=100 (range 10-52), ~6 at 2,430 (range 0.3-37, extrapolated); lower number does not mean DFT became more useful. [OWN-EXPERIMENT, corrected framing]
- Minami 2025 (polymers sim->real): E = C + D*N^-alpha with floor "transfer gap"; each 10x simulation removes only 1.4-7.8% of error; ~221 simulations ~ 1 experiment, only locally (~71k sims, ~600 expts). Lee & Asahi 2021: 0/10k/54k/113k -> 0.978/0.866/0.826/0.815 eV (DFT target; last step within ~0.09 run spread). Devi 2024: non-monotonic (10k helped less than 5k or 50k). ElemNet on DFT itself: ~0.35 -> 0.05 eV/atom from 464 -> 231k. [SOURCE]
- Floors: calorimetry ~0.023 eV/atom; compilations differ ~0.030; two databases disagree 0.082 on 75 intermetallics (Kirklin 2015); DFT vs experiment ~0.08-0.10. REFUTED/softened: "Jha's 0.07 is already in the noise range" not established (Jha's set is mostly oxides, nitrides, hydrides, halides, no error bars). [SOURCE]
- Extrapolation unreliable: Chen spans 1.6 decades; fitted floor anywhere from 0 to ~best observed; at N=2,430 three shapes predict 0.37-0.41 eV at 410k (band 0.26-0.47); predicted 41k->410k gain 0.009-0.040 eV vs minimum detectable ~0.08 eV (~270 test compounds, six paired repeats; 0.187 single runs); 20k->41k at 2,430 not significant (p=0.15); 10k->20k went the wrong way (0.43->0.45). Toy simulation: backtest success ~uncorrelated with 100x extrapolation error (bias 0.02-0.18 eV). Test-set sampling limit for ~1,643-2k formation-energy compounds: 0.002-0.007 eV/atom (corrected: sampling noise, not measurement noise). REFUTED: "don't extrapolate past ~10x" rule — unsupported. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- Premise "obvious because data scarce" is half right: needs relatedness (Devi), pretraining can hurt small models with plentiful fine-tune data (Hernandez, text->code), overlap inflates gains (Chen random splits put same structures in both sides). Possible hidden couplings (plausible, not measured): OQMD elemental references and MP corrections fitted to the same experimental data used for testing. Counterpoint: Gong 2022 removed test compounds from pretraining, transfer still helped (~0.075 vs 0.104, figure-read). [SOURCE/OPINION]
- Raw count is the wrong x-axis: only ~13-55% of big DFT databases informative; well-chosen 20% matches random 50-90% (Li 2023); ~250k of ~300k OQMD entries are hypothetical prototype decorations; a model scoring 0.022 eV/atom in-distribution scored 0.297 on newer alloys. [SOURCE]

## 5. Problems / gaps identified
- Atlas grid lacks definitions of present/partial and a roll-up rule; two inconsistent rules change a headline count (6 of 9 -> 4 of 9) — hurts anyone reusing the atlas as a data-quality reference. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- RRUFF: no units/axis names in arrays, "RAW" already processed, no array uncertainty, no cross-database ids, no license — hurts ML users wanting quantitative intensities or automated joins. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- No shared public identifier between experimental and computed records (only ICSD ids; structures not public); joins depend on structure matching that hides volume and handedness differences. [OWN/SOURCE]
- Benchmark labels without provenance: Matbench quartz e_form -3.2741 has no id/version and is irreproducible; one MP summary record silently mixes functionals. [OWN-EXPERIMENT]
- DFT data carry no uncertainty (null); experimental thermochemistry/band gaps have spread and few error bars. [SOURCE]
- No published clean learning curve of experimental formation-energy error vs DFT pretraining size (size confounded with database everywhere). [SOURCE/OPINION]
- Experimental test sets (~270 to ~2k) too small to detect predicted gains; leakage/duplicates between DFT train and experimental test already changed published numbers once. [OWN/SOURCE]
- Hull position is a poor proxy for "exists" in both directions. [SOURCE]

## 10. Jargon that matters
- RRUFF — public database of measured mineral specimens (Raman, XRD, IR, microprobe); a small labelled "real-world" dataset.
- DFT — quantum simulation of an ideal crystal at 0 K; the "synthetic data" of materials.
- Functional (PBE/GGA, r2SCAN, HSE06) — the approximation inside DFT; like different simulators with different systematic bias.
- Formation energy — energy released forming a compound from elements; main regression label.
- Convex hull / energy above hull — predicted-stability score; not a synthesizability label.
- MP2020 correction — per-element offsets fitted to experiment; label leakage from experiment into "DFT".
- Unit cell / esd — crystal's repeating box / its reported standard uncertainty, e.g. 4.9134(2).
- Enantiomorph (#152 vs #154) — mirror-image crystal forms; a join-key trap.
- StructureMatcher — pymatgen fuzzy structure join; rescales volume before comparing.
- Multi-fidelity / transfer / delta-learning — ways to train on cheap DFT + scarce experiment with source labels.
- Transfer gap (floor C) — irreducible error when pretraining on simulation; asymptote of the curve.
- Minimum detectable difference — smallest MAE gap a test set of given size can resolve.
- JCAMP-DX / CIF / GUM / FAIR / Porto — community standards for spectra files, crystal files, uncertainty, data sharing, Raman geometry.
- Polymorph — same formula, different structure; invisible to composition-only models (ElemNet).

## 12. Smart or non-obvious ideas worth not losing
- A data-quality grid is only credible with written state definitions and a roll-up rule; the null-vs-absent choice alone moves a headline statistic (6/9 -> 4/9).
- "Corrected DFT" labels are partly fitted to the same experimental data used for testing — a leakage channel nobody has measured.
- The clean DFT-size learning curve for experimental formation energy (size isolated from database, leakage controlled, seeds repeated) appears unpublished and is buildable from public data.
- Before promising extrapolation, compute the minimum detectable difference of the experimental test set: here predicted gains (0.009-0.040 eV) sit below detectability (~0.08 eV), so bigger/cleaner experimental test sets are the real bottleneck.
- Raw count is the wrong x-axis: redundancy (13-55% informative) and hypothetical prototypes mean curated/diverse DFT subsets may beat "more DFT".
