## 10. Jargon
- DFT / Materials Project (MP): simulated properties — synthetic labels, not measurements.
- Matbench: standard ML benchmark suite, mostly computed labels.
- Starrydata / teMatDb / ESTM: thermoelectric datasets digitised from paper figures (human-annotated labels).
- zT, Seebeck (S), resistivity, thermal conductivity, power factor (PF): linked TE quantities; zT = S^2*T/(rho*kappa), so records can be self-checked like a checksum.
- Physical identity check: recompute one curve from others; mismatch = label error.
- Compound error: two curves wrong at once, so the culprit cannot be localised.
- Paper-grouped split: GroupKFold by DOI to stop leakage.
- Pre-registration / matched null: fix metric and pass/fail before looking; compare every flag to a shuffled baseline.
- Precursor Genome (PG): autonomous-lab synthesis ledger (precursors, furnace log, masses, XRD outcome).
- GPSS / Dara supplement: A-Lab XRD set; weighed-mixture scans with known answers.
- RRUFF / opXRD: mineral reference patterns / pooled open experimental XRD.
- Calculated vs measured pattern: simulated from a crystal structure vs recorded on an instrument (synthetic vs real image).
- Handedness / mirror copies: left/right-handed structures; indistinguishable in powder XRD.
- Wilson / clustered bootstrap CI: intervals that respect that samples from one paper are not independent.

## 12. Smart or non-obvious ideas worth not losing
1. Use a second, independent curation of the same paper as a free label-noise estimate AND as the "known-good" null for scoring any linter.
2. The >10% disagreement tail is identity/unit errors, not noise — so score benchmarks with a median metric plus a gross-error-removed variant, and ship the error list as errata.
3. Join on figure labels, not composition; store the tolerance with every join; normalise DOIs first.
4. Grouping unit for fair splits is dataset-specific: paper for TE, precursor for PG (chemical system is a no-op).
5. A file-only "is this pattern calculated?" check (97.4% agreement) can lint XRD training sets without trusting headers — opXRD already absorbed 414 calculated RRUFF profiles labelled as measured.
