## is eval work: mostly

Test sets and answer keys: D1 (the 40-row marking test and the tricky-pairs key), D6 and N3 (harder public weighed sets as a practice exam), L1 (specialist check of the key), L2 and L5 (hidden exams). NOT: D0 file rescue is housekeeping. D2 (499 files that look simulated but are filed as measured) is provenance curation. D5, L3 and L4 (trimmed error notes, neutral notice) are error reporting to dataset owners. N6 (thermoelectric suspect list, flag-only) is data quality control. These are the items closest to the user's stated focus, and the plan ranks all of them as 'courtesy', 'side deliverable' or 'lowest priority'. Note: evaluation DATA is itself curation (choosing, checking and documenting measured test items). The earlier decision 'evaluation data before a training corpus' (storyline.md:63) is a curation decision, not a departure from it.
NAME: Auditing and repairing the exams for ML on measured materials data: the test items, answer keys, marking rules and splits. In short, evaluation-data curation plus exam auditing, with some eval-about-eval tooling on the side.
