# Thermal paste completion checklist

Updated 12 October 2026. **Finish one bounded application study: two freshly and independently prepared batches of the existing recipe, plus purchased DOWSIL 340.** Use [PASTE_TO_LAB_RUN_CARD.md](PASTE_TO_LAB_RUN_CARD.md), method PASTE-P2-v1, for the complete hands-on instructions. The recipe and timed sequence are unchanged. This checklist replaces the older mixed LAB/EXP task list; previous versions remain in Git history.

The questions are: how much thermal resistance does this formulation introduce under specified conditions, how do two preparations compare, and how do both compare with the measured commercial reference? Two batches provide preliminary evidence, not a general reproducibility estimate, a qualified chip product or proof of model-training value.

## 1 Agree on the laboratory work

- [ ] **G2a. Prepare and send the service form after reviewing it.** Use [the technical form guide](CALLS_AND_ORDERS.md#longwin-service-form-next-action). Scope: two independent homemade preparations and DOWSIL 340, three materials total. A fourth specimen is optional and separately quoted; it is not required.
- [ ] **G2b. Request the base measurement scope.** Thermal impedance at three engineer-selected thicknesses per material, with apparent conductivity fitted only if the data support it. Request one independent reload at a common comparison condition for each material. Ask the engineer to select feasible thickness, pressure and temperature conditions for the actual powders, explain gap versus pressure control, and report the actual values. Do not prescribe gaps from nominal particle size alone.
- [ ] **G2c. Set deliverables before paying.** Ask for numerical Excel/CSV results, thickness, area, load/pressure, temperatures, conditioning/stability information, repeat results and uncertainty. Ask separately which raw time-series can be delivered, and obtain agreement on publishing the results and identifying the laboratory. The [public service page](https://longwinusa.com/services/thermal-interface-material-test/) advertises Excel capability, but project-specific delivery needs confirmation.
- [ ] **G2d. Agree logistics.** Obtain accepted container, sample quantity for the final scope, age/storage/remixing instructions, quote/payment terms, drop-off and result dates. The earlier amount was approximately 1 mL per specimen; proposing 5 g is a surplus allowance, not a validated volume conversion or lab requirement. Do not make submission batches before these preparation requirements are settled.

If the quote is too high, request a reduced scope: all three materials at one useful matched condition with independent reloads, omitting conductivity extraction. Decide before testing. Do not substitute a final conductivity number alone for the comparative point data.

## 2 Prepare the bench without opening powder

- [ ] **S1/S2. Confirm the existing supplies.** Oil: Super Lube 56404. Coarse: McMaster 5092N124. Fine: 5092N131. Reference: DOWSIL 340. Record lots and available certificates. Ask McMaster for SDSs explicitly matched to the two purchased powder part numbers; the linked tabular/calcined family sheets have not yet been conclusively matched to delivered lots.
- [ ] **G1b. Resolve the actual handling arrangement.** Cover weighing, powder transfer, mixing and cleanup for **two 25.010 g batches: 36.600 g total alumina**. Document the SDS-based workspace, dust-control and protective-equipment choices. A loose bag or a balance cover does not establish airborne-dust control in the earlier shared living room. If that is still the available setup, arrange preparation at a lab that explicitly accepts these powders and the work, or obtain competent guidance on a suitable arrangement. Maintain necessary dust control while weighing. The deferred 1.3 kg campaign is outside this scope.
- [ ] **S3/S4. Assemble and dry the kit.** Two matching lidded mixing vessels, one spatula, one rigid rest that fits with the spatula fully on the balance, two dedicated dry powder scoops, one reference-only transfer tool, accepted submission containers, labels, marker, timer, tray, wipes, protective equipment and suitable waste containers. Use existing suitable vessels if the rehearsal passes; approximately 60 mL smooth-sided cups are an option, not a compulsory new purchase.
- [ ] **S5a. Check the balance.** Warm, level and calibrate according to its manual. With documented-tolerance weights or combinations near 2 g, 5 g and 20 g, record five place/remove readings each and zero returns. Then repeat the 2 g check with the chosen empty cup on the pan and tared. Do not use a jar or a coin as a known calibration mass.
- [ ] **S5b. Assess the actual readings.** Pilot working limits: absolute indication error plus stated weight tolerance at most 0.010 g; each five-reading range at most 0.010 g. Record stable zero returns and configuration. These limits are not a calibration certificate. If checks fail, resolve placement, drafts, level, warm-up or cover/contact issues and repeat. Never switch off required dust control to stabilize readings. The previous 200 g calibration/practice exception did not verify small additions.
- [ ] **B1. Dry-rehearse the complete arrangement.** Check tool reach to cup bottom/walls, stable cup-only weighing, separate tool/rest weighing without external contact, and gross loads below the 500 g capacity even after tare. Use this exact arrangement for both batches.
- [ ] **S6. Prepare records.** Check unused batch IDs; B002/B003 are provisional. Print one [batch sheet](../../trajectory/batch-sheet.md) per run and the run card. Record intended method, specimen role and timing before starting. Mark unrelated density/spread fields not measured. If forecasts will be evaluated, preserve them before the relevant outcomes, explicitly acknowledging that B001 is already known. Forecasting is not required to purchase a useful thermal comparison.

**No additional oil, powders, balance, mixer, curing kit or thermal rig is required by this scope.** Borrow documented check weights if possible. Only replace or obtain missing items above.

## 3 Prepare two independent specimens

Proceed when G2, G1b, S5 and B1 are complete. Follow the [full run card](PASTE_TO_LAB_RUN_CARD.md) without waiting for chat approval between routine additions.

| Ingredient | Target per batch | Total for both batches |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Oil | 6.710 g | 13.420 g |
| Coarse alumina | 4.270 g × 3 = 12.810 g | 25.620 g |
| Fine alumina | 1.830 g × 3 = 5.490 g | 10.980 g |
| Total | **25.010 g** | **50.020 g** |

- [ ] Record the empty labeled cup baseline without lid/tool, and the clean spatula-plus-rest baseline separately.
- [ ] Fresh cup-only tare before each addition: oil once, then three coarse/fine pairs. Record actual readings separately from targets. Mix off the balance.
- [ ] Mix 60 seconds after each pair, followed by another 180 seconds after the third pair: **360 seconds active mixing total**. Fold/press and gather wall coatings into the bulk; avoid whisking or wiping paste onto the upper rim. Park the spatula on its original rest without wiping between rounds.
- [ ] Rest covered for 120 seconds. Record surface photo, one channel/lift, and a fixed-view 60-second video; note channel behavior at 10/60 seconds, visible dry pockets/bubbles/oil and handling feel. These are descriptive observations, not viscosity measurements or proof of uniformity.
- [ ] Before sampling or cleanup, clear old tare with the pan empty and separately weigh cup-plus-paste and tool-plus-rest-plus-residue. Calculate mass accounting exactly as on the run card. Cup-wall coatings already count in cup mass. Residue mass cannot reveal its oil/powder composition; **do not adjust the recipe to compensate**.
- [ ] Cap, label and record storage. Repeat from fresh ingredients in the second vessel, after cleaning/drying reusable tools. Use the same method and material lots; record dates and ages. Splitting one jar does not create two preparations.

Use the existing ±0.020 g per-addition working target. Keep actual overshoots and method deviations in the record; do not hide them by scooping mixed material out or adding a guessed correction. Stiffness or a poor result is not an exclusion reason. Stop affected work for a spill/dust-control problem, unidentified ingredient or contamination; preserve the event and seek a recorded method decision before proceeding. If the method changes between batches, report them as different methods rather than an exact repeat pair.

## 4 Pack and submit

- [ ] Leave the commercial reference unmodified; use its separate tool only if transfer is requested.
- [ ] Pack each material according to the laboratory's instructions. Weigh each empty destination with its lid, then sealed with paste; the difference is net contents. Record parent batch, specimen ID, contents, dates, net amount and any remixing or transfer. If the lab accepts the original mixing vessel, avoid unnecessary transfer.
- [ ] Keep retained material capped, upright, in labeled secondary containment at stable indoor room temperature away from food, sunlight and heaters, subject to agreed lab instructions. Record actual conditions. This is provisional storage, not validated shelf life; do not heat, refrigerate, dilute or remix without a method decision.
- [ ] Deliver only after acceptance, with specimen manifest, actual composition, SDSs, ages and relevant deviations. Obtain receipt and expected result date. Preserve raw preparation records privately and back them up; only explicitly authorized records/photos are published.

B001 remains an aged practice archive. Photograph its closed jar and record any visible layer without disturbing it. It can be an optional extra test specimen if accepted and quoted, but it does not replace either new preparation. Do not thin or remix it to make it look better.

## 5 Finish the study and decide once

- [ ] Save laboratory originals unchanged and link every result to its specimen and parent batch.
- [ ] Compare the two homemade batches and the measured reference at comparable actual thickness/pressure/temperature, using reload variation and reported uncertainty. If conditions differ, show the differences rather than forcing a ranking.
- [ ] Preserve failed loading, poor thermal results and deviations. Three thicknesses × three materials yields nine measurement conditions; one extra reload each would yield approximately twelve point results. There are still only **two independently prepared homemade batches**.
- [ ] Write a short report: what was made, measured application performance, differences versus repeat/measurement variation, limitations, and the next decision. A conductivity fit alone does not qualify this paste for valuable electronics or prove the value of richer experimental histories.

Stop this paste milestone after the report. Additional formulation optimization needs a specific question supported by the results; it is not automatic. Wafer-film access inquiries can proceed while laboratory work is scheduled. Expanded titration/settling experiments, cured composites and home-rig construction remain deferred.
