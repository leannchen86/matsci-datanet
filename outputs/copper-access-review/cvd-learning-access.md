## Suggested learning sequence

1. **Wafer-film deposition and measurement:** execute an approved oxide or nitride PECVD recipe, then measure thickness and uniformity. Repeat before varying a staff-approved parameter. The product is a real deposited film; electrical performance requires separate measurements.
2. **Diamond-film growth:** learn the diamond tool's preparation, approved substrates and standard process; characterize the deposit with appropriate microscopy, thickness measurement and Raman analysis. This is a separate qualification with potentially long growth cycles.
3. **Interface coatings:** first consider a planar diamond specimen to study an approved metal/carbide coating. Then assess particle coating with an appropriate fixture and contamination approval. PVD may be the appropriate method. None of the verified reactor listings establishes permission to process loose diamond powder.

This sequence is a planning recommendation, not a facility-prescribed curriculum or guaranteed timeline. For the Cu/diamond project, particle coating and growth of diamond itself solve different problems. Buying documented diamond feedstock remains an option while learning growth separately.
