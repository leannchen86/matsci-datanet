# Palo Alto and flexible laboratory access

Checked 11 October 2026. **Shared chemistry laboratories deserve priority alongside specialist facilities. Zikon advertises day access in Sunnyvale; AcmeBay is the strongest Palo Alto chemistry lead found, but its short-term access remains unconfirmed.** No facility has accepted the project or quoted a complete experiment. These findings extend the [Bay Area comparison](README.md).

## What changes in our approach

The earlier search stayed too closely tied to the original AlN reproduction and Stanford's equipment. Moving to paste preparation or copper electrodeposition should have triggered a fresh facility comparison. A shared chemistry bench may suit those operations, subject to process approval, even when specialist deposition or measurement equipment must be accessed elsewhere.

For this pilot, seek a short, assisted preparation session and independently price the required measurements. Compare complete experiment costs and usable records. A cheap bench without the right controller, training or measurements may cost more overall than a staffed process service. This is a recommendation, not a confirmed booking model at any particular facility.

## Day-pass benchmark and remaining requirements

[Zikon](https://www.zikon.com/lab.html), 365 San Aleso Ave in **Sunnyvale**, advertises **$75–150/day** for a shared bench and scheduled equipment use, including basic safety orientation. PhD consultation hours are explicitly included in its monthly bench tier; day-pass supervision is not established. Its public capabilities do not identify a copper controller, cell or film-metrology instruments. A day pass is an access price, not a complete pilot quote.

For each candidate, obtain answers to these project-specific questions before booking:

- Can an individual use the facility, and what are the minimum term, onboarding requirements and actual first available date?
- Will staff approve the proposed copper bath, rinsing, storage and waste route? For paste work, will they approve fine-alumina handling and the available containment?
- Which named instruments are available and included? For copper: a logging potentiostat/galvanostat, cell, electrodes and sample holder; for paste: suitable weighing and mixing equipment.
- Is an experienced person available to review the protocol and assist with the first session? General orientation alone does not establish experiment supervision.
- Where will thickness, roughness and electrical measurements happen, and what sample geometry do those instruments require?
- What is the total first-session price, including required training, staff time, instruments, consumables, storage and waste? Can we export and publish the agreed run records?

For advanced wafer processing, confirm the actual deposition/patterning tools and contamination rules separately. General wet-lab access does not establish a cleanroom process or semiconductor qualification.
