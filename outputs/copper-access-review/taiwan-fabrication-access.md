# Taiwan wafer fabrication access and costs

Checked 12 October 2026. **Taiwan is a credible alternative for paid wafer processing and structured practical courses. Lower total cost and faster independent hands-on access remain conditional.** Prioritize Hsinchu: TSRI, NYCU and NTHU provide several relevant routes in one city. No institution has accepted this project, supplied a quote or confirmed an appointment.

For CVD and wafer doping, search for semiconductor cleanrooms and process services. A wet-chemistry bench rental does not include a deposition reactor or ion implanter. Stanford is also not the sole California option: [Berkeley Marvell](https://nanolab.berkeley.edu/public/capabilities/capabilities.shtml) lists PECVD, LPCVD and diamond CVD.

TSRI's [self-operation policy](https://www.tsri.org.tw/tw/commonPage.jsp?kindId=F0005) requires academic project authorization, relevant courses, safety and tool qualification. Its separate [commissioned-service procedure](https://www.tsri.org.tw/tw/commonPage.jsp?kindId=F0006) starts with engineer review, followed by membership/MES application, quotation, sample submission and billing. Paying for processing does not establish permission to operate the tool.

NYCU explicitly provides a [service route without an NSTC grant](https://nanofc.web.nycu.edu.tw/委託代工申請/). Its [FAQ](https://nanofc.web.nycu.edu.tw/常見問題/) allows outside applicants, while the [application procedure](https://nanofc.web.nycu.edu.tw/奈米中心申請流程/) requires supporting qualifications. The FAQ's three-business-day review is not a promise of complete onboarding within three days.

NTU's [service portal](https://nems.ntu.edu.tw/OEM/) permits accompanied processing for up to two people, with cleanroom charges. A specified appointment or completion within five working days counts as rush work at twice the instrument operation fee, subject to capacity. Its 卓越 PECVD service notice still says suspended from 10 February 2026, with reopening expected after October; confirm restoration rather than assuming it has happened. The separate SPSR ICP-CVD listing is not proof of a free slot.

## Two concrete studies to quote

**First choice for learning and a usable process record: dielectric-film deposition.** Ask NYCU/NTHU for a standard SiO₂ or silicon-nitride PECVD process on their approved silicon substrates. Propose two staff-approved conditions plus one repeat, with thickness/uniformity measurements and permitted recipe/run records. The lab should define feasible substrates, sequence and minimum billing before purchase. This produces actual wafer films and a small reproducibility dataset. Thickness alone does not establish electrical dielectric quality; electrical testing would require a suitable test structure.

**Doping alternative: implantation followed by activation and electrical measurement.** Ask TSRI for a small silicon-wafer study including starting-wafer specification, agreed dose/energy conditions, annealing, and sheet-resistance measurements. Request untreated controls and repeat measurements. [CF-S10's service specification](https://www.tsri.org.tw/CommonUtilServlet?file=S10_C.PDF&type=2.84) supports common semiconductor dopant species over defined size/energy ranges. Its [sample rules](https://www.tsri.org.tw/CommonUtilServlet?file=S10_D.PDF&type=2.84) require suitable carriers and engineer approval for nonstandard specimens. Do not buy arbitrary small pieces first.

Depositing an in-situ doped polysilicon film is another process, distinct from implanting a silicon wafer. Silicon implantation also does not establish diamond-doping capability. No fast independent diamond-growth or hydrogen-termination route in Taiwan was verified by this comparison.

**Decision:** pursue TSRI course eligibility and NYCU/NTHU small-service quotes in parallel. Travel becomes a grounded choice once one provider accepts the payer, project, dates and data scope. No outreach or booking has been made.
