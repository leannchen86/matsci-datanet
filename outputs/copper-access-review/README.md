## Scope to obtain comparable quotes

Request a small pilot consisting of **two current-density settings with three independently executed depositions each**, plus an unplated seed-layer control and an optional commissioning specimen. Six coupons processed together are not six independent depositions. Final dimensions, currents, chemistry and endpoint must be set with the operator and analyst.

Request pricing for:

1. Project review, onboarding, training and an assisted first session.
2. Prepared conductive coupons with documented layer stack and baseline thickness; an insulating support beneath the conductive seed prevents substrate conduction from obscuring electrical results.
3. The cell, holder, electrodes, controller, bath, rinsing, drying and waste collection.
4. Linked actual current/voltage/time files, bath preparation and age, temperature, exposed area, specimen preparation, interruptions and unsuccessful runs. Fix or record agitation and geometry. Equal electrical charge does not guarantee equal copper mass.
5. Thickness at defined locations and surface roughness, plus sheet resistance if the geometry and instrument range permit meaningful interpretation of the seed-plus-deposit stack. Price a separate adhesion test if needed; a visual check is not quantified adhesion.
6. Earliest start/results dates, minimum billing, taxes, cancellation terms and permission to publish agreed records. A proprietary recipe that cannot be disclosed may not fit the data goal even if the coating is excellent.

This is an initial process study, not product qualification or evidence that models benefit from expanded histories. The [earlier robot study](https://luo.chem.utah.edu/publications/a-modular-and-affordable-self-driving-laboratory-for-vision-guided-optimization-of-metal-electrodeposition.pdf) optimized optical darkness of copper on graphite. The wafer/conductivity study is a deliberate adaptation.

For independent characterization, [Covalent's profilometry](https://covalent.com/resource-library/optical-microscopy-and-profilometry-services/) and [four-point services](https://covalent.com/techniques/more-techniques/4-point-probe-4pp-measurement/) are local possibilities. Its [FAQ](https://covalent.com/faqs/) gives quote-based pricing and about five to seven business days for many in-house services, subject to scope. Confirm individual acceptance, copper sensitivity and coupon geometry before fabrication.

## Useful work with existing paste materials

The fastest route to an application measurement is to quote **existing B001 plus DOWSIL 340** as two exploratory specimens, subject to the lab accepting B001's age, condition and container. This adds no fresh powder work. It cannot establish preparation repeatability. The current [paste run card](../thermal-paste-first-cycle/PASTE_TO_LAB_RUN_CARD.md) instead provides two fresh independent batches plus the commercial reference if repeatability is part of the funded scope.

LongWin's October 6 response is summarized in [calls and orders](../thermal-paste-first-cycle/CALLS_AND_ORDERS.md): complete the service form before a quote; roughly 1 mL per paste; indicative one week testing plus one week reporting; no confirmed price/date. Numerical files and publication permission remain to be agreed for the actual job. The earlier **$1,000** figure is our spending target, not a quote. Private correspondence is not reproduced here.

Ask for thermal impedance under comparable measured thickness, pressure and temperature; price a three-thickness conductivity fit and fresh reloads separately. A confirmed test protocol can then support a deliberate filler-loading or coarse:fine ratio study with existing oil and alumina. Those ingredients alone are not a curing system for solid pads. Fresh preparations still require resolving the handling and measurement checks in the run card.
