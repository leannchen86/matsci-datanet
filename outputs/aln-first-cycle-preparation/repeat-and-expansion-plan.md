# Inventory, independent repeats, and additional papers

Proposed revision, **4 October 2026**, following the user's interest in continued and parallel experiments. No additional experiment or purchase has been booked. The $15,000 ceiling and October 25 target remain unchanged.

## Procurement recommendation

Request prices for **one, two and three matching research wafers**, preferably from the same supplier lot and oxide process. **Two is the recommended starting inventory**, once the stack is accepted: wafer W01 for the first work, W02 kept in protected storage for replacement or later experiments. A third is conditional on a named follow-on use or a purchasing advantage. Retain the existing substrate/preparation allowance until real quotes justify a revision.

The tool's approved carrier is a separate item; obtain it from Stanford if possible. Do not count a carrier as a spare research substrate. Coupon dimensions, usable area, cutting losses and surface requirements determine yield. One wafer can supply multiple coupon batches, but no particular yield is guaranteed in this plan.

## Count the experimental units correctly

| Unit | What it represents | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Parent wafer | Purchased substrate stock and provenance | One wafer does not mean one experiment |
| Coupon | A cut specimen used for deposition or measurement | Several coupons in one deposition are not independent runs |
| Deposition run | A separately executed process event with its own recipe, actuals and deviations | Another measurement spot is not another deposition |
| Independent repeat | Fresh coupons, same intended recipe, separate deposition; history recorded | Two runs alone do not establish robust reproducibility |
| Paper-based experiment | A selected claim/condition with a defined stack, equipment and measurement comparison | Purchasing the same material name does not reproduce a paper |

## A small campaign to quote, in stages

1. **R01 — first documented run.** Preserve the existing scope: one agreed recipe, two or three same-run coupons, thickness/texture and one thermal specimen. Resolve data-export and measurement problems here.
2. **R02 — optional independent repeat.** Obtain an incremental quote now for a separate run of the same intended recipe on fresh coupons, ideally from the same parent wafer/lot. Include its own structural measurements, transducer and thermal specimen if thermal consistency is the question. Release only when R01's records and measurement route are usable and the remaining budget and confirmed schedule cover it. A low valid conductivity result is not automatically a reason to cancel a repeat. If the recipe changes, label R02 an iteration, not a repeat.
3. **R03 — optional controlled variation.** After reviewing the first results, choose one staff-approved process variable and a question it can answer. Quote before executing. No parameter value or additional run is authorized by this plan.

R02 need not wait for every line of the final R01 report if acquisition, sample preparation and records are already demonstrably usable and the provider supports the same analysis path. Agree that readiness point explicitly; do not blindly repeat a known sample-preparation or measurement failure. R01 remains the committed planning scope; R02/R03 are options to cost and schedule, not promised October 25 deliverables.
