**Preferred:** ask the selected fabrication facility to supply the accepted substrate and its provenance, or to approve a supplier specification before the user orders. This avoids buying an incompatible wafer and adding unnecessary dicing/cleaning steps.

| **Hionix** as fabricator-supplier | [Film service page](https://hionix.com/pvd-films/) offers wafers/oxidation; published dry oxide starts at **500 Å = 50 nm** | Exact 14 nm would be custom/unverified or supplied externally; do not substitute its standard oxide silently |

**Quote specification:** Si(100); polished deposition face; nominal 14 nm thermally grown SiO2; actual thickness evidence/tolerance; wafer thickness, doping/resistivity and lot documented; dimensions chosen by the fabrication and measurement providers; two or three same-wafer coupons if feasible. Ask whether oxide is on one or both sides. Avoid ordering extra backside processing without a tool/model reason. Record deviations explicitly.

A stock 100 nm or 300 nm oxide wafer is a changed thermal stack, not an equivalent substitute. Growing thermal oxide before AlN does not establish or violate a later AlN deposition-temperature claim; record the stages separately. The facility should supply its approved Al target, process gases, cleaning consumables and mounting hardware. No founder purchase of cylinders, solvents or an unqualified target is proposed.

## Measurement purchase conditions

Before work starts, obtain a named owner for transducer preparation, layer-thickness inputs, any calibration reference, raw data and final fitting. Ask for a line-item price including failed/inconclusive acquisition handling, sample returns, shipping, taxes and rush charges. An attractive measurement price without fit inputs and usable record delivery may not satisfy this pilot.

Ask for a delivery date measured from **accepted sample receipt**, plus the latest acceptable receipt date for an October 25 result. Do not convert advertised turnaround into a reservation. Local handoff can remove shipping days, but must use the provider's agreed intake process and receiving location.
