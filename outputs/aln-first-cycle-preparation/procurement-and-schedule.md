# First AlN cycle: procurement, dates, and spending controls

Prepared **Sunday, October 4, 2026**. Location: **California**; specialist testing elsewhere is acceptable if needed. The user's first preference is **hands-on nano@stanford access as a solo researcher**, with paid fabrication as a fallback. Desired closeout: **Sunday, October 25, 2026**. Total budget ceiling: **US$15,000**. Individual versus company billing is still unresolved. Registration assistance is authorized; this document does not authorize purchases, paid work, signatures, or bookings. No supplier has accepted this scope or committed to these dates.

## What this cycle buys

One chosen, executable reactive **DC** sputter recipe producing nominally **200 nm AlN on approximately 14 nm thermal SiO₂ / Si(100)**, with actual substrate temperature below 200°C under an agreed verification method. Make **2–3 uniquely identified coupons in the same deposition**, measure AlN thickness and texture before coating, then prepare nominally **80 nm Al** on the primary coupon and perform TDTR at **three locations on that one specimen**. Keep an uncoated companion. These are not independent deposition repeats.

The output is a linked fabrication-and-measurement record, including uncertainty and any unsuccessful or inconclusive outcome. One cycle cannot validate the causal claim that unpublished experimental histories accelerate research. The detailed technical acceptance conditions and record list are in [research-scope.md](research-scope.md).

## Critical decisions before ordering

1. **Confirm a contracting path and actual access.** Ask Stanford administration whether an unaffiliated individual can contract or whether an organization is required. Complete the appropriate external account, agreement, financial authorization, safety onboarding, and tool qualification. Account creation is not tool authorization or a reserved slot. Tool training can be scheduled only after lab access is active. Published account setup can take a couple of days and up to ten business days at peaks. [External access](https://nanolabs.stanford.edu/access/external), [Allen safety onboarding](https://nanolabs.stanford.edu/equipment/allensafety), [PO requirements](https://nanolabs.stanford.edu/access/external/purchase-order).
2. **Obtain the thermal provider's written stack and analysis acceptance first.** Confirm dimensions, substrate thickness, roughness/handling requirements, who deposits and characterizes Al, and whether this stack supports a defensible AlN conductivity estimate rather than only a combined thermal resistance. Agree fitted versus fixed interface terms and input uncertainties. Covalent is a California inquiry lead for coordinated TDTR, XRR, and XRD; capability advertising does not establish acceptance or a rush slot.
3. **Agree a common fabrication-to-measurement format.** A provisional 20 × 20 mm coupon is not a universal specification. Hionix publishes a nominal minimum silicon sample size of **25 mm**, while Covalent's TDTR page recommends approximately **10 × 10 mm**. Before purchase, settle the deposition carrier, metrology holders, dicing/cleaving method and timing, whether parent samples must be larger, who performs any cutting, and how the child specimen maps to the original run. Do not assume either published size is a hard limit or an already-approved exception. [Hionix specifications](https://hionix.com/pvd-films/), [Covalent TDTR](https://covalent.com/techniques/thermal-analysis/time-domain-thermoreflectance-tdtr-analysis/).
4. **Choose one fabrication route before committing production costs.** Prefer Stanford only if training, staff approval, a suitable DC AlN recipe/target, and the tool slot can meet the schedule. Confirm whether Al is installed now: the [Lesker 2 manual](https://snfguide.stanford.edu/guide/docs/operating-instructions/lesker2-operation-manual) describes target changes every two weeks and at least six hours of pumpdown after a change. Staff must confirm the current cadence; this is not a bookable calendar. Otherwise obtain a fixed-scope paid-fabrication offer, initially from Hionix; obtain the user's choice before switching away from hands-on work. Do not run duplicate production orders merely to hedge availability.

## Terms to settle with each provider

- Itemized included work, minimum charges, handling/setup/analysis time, overhead/tax, rush premium, what triggers additional fees, and a named person authorized to approve changes. Confirm cancellation/rescheduling deadlines, nonrefundable custom-material charges, refunds/credits after provider delay, and charges for unusable or inconclusive samples; do not assume a successful conductivity extraction is guaranteed.
- Written delivery of the existing recipe/log exports, sample-ID manifest, raw thickness/texture/TDTR data, fit inputs/results and uncertainty explanation. Set a date for the record package as well as for the measurement. If essential recipe or raw files cannot be released, resolve that before ordering; a summary certificate alone does not meet the record-linkage objective.
- Provider-approved packing, return/shipping arrangements, sample-retention period and permission for destructive preparation. Keep B and optional C uncoated unless an agreed measurement requires otherwise. Photograph/inventory specimens at each handoff and preserve parent–child IDs if cut after deposition.
- A completed poor-performing film is still useful if its measurement is valid. A fabrication or metrology failure is documented in its own category. Neither automatically authorizes another deposition, a thickness series, extra microscopy, or consumption of the reserve.
