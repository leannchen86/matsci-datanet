**AlN first experimental cycle — hands-on nano@stanford scope and staffed alternative**

Updated 4 October 2026. Primary route: the user leads one project and performs hands-on work at nano@stanford after the required training and qualification. Staff-operated work is the alternate for steps that cannot be qualified or scheduled in time. Planning budget: **USD 15,000**. Target: **close the first experimental cycle by 25 October 2026**. Registration assistance is authorized; this file records no completed registration, training, reservation, purchase or experiment.

**Purpose and boundary.** Produce one documented deposition realization and determine whether its cross-plane thermal response yields a usable AlN conductivity estimate. Use one executable recipe on two or three coupons, with one coupon receiving TDTR. This cycle tests the fabrication-to-measurement workflow and record linkage. It does not test whether an expanded historical archive accelerates research, establish reproducibility across depositions, or promise the paper's best performance.

The Stanford paper provides process windows, not an exact four-parameter recipe explicitly linked to its best result. Its SI describes an **AJA ATC 1800-F** reactive DC sputtering system, standard approximately 14 nm SiO₂/Si(100) substrates, and mostly 100–300 nm films. It uses an 80 nm Al transducer and three TDTR locations per sample. These are reference choices for this scope, not proof of transfer to another instrument. [Paper and SI, physical PDF pp. 10–11, S1](https://poplab.stanford.edu/pdfs/Vaziri-AlNthermalMaterial3DICs-afm25.pdf).

**Stanford tool choice and process transfer.** The public [Lesker](https://snfguide.stanford.edu/guide/equipment/lesker-sputter-lesker-sputter) and [Lesker2](https://snfguide.stanford.edu/guide/equipment/lesker2-sputter-lesker2-sputter) pages list Al, substrate pieces and reactive N₂ sputtering. Lesker is classified as flexible/gold-contaminated; Lesker2 is semiclean. These listings make them candidates, but do not establish an available, qualified AlN recipe or the particular DC operating mode needed here. Have the responsible staff choose the compatible tool and approve coupon/holder materials and downstream metrology access. A facility cleanliness classification is not a measurement of contamination in a particular film.

A Lesker run is an **adapted low-temperature AlN pilot**, not an exact reproduction of the AJA study. Before execution, document the selected tool's cathode/power mode, target material and dimensions, geometry, usable target distance, gas delivery, deposition-rate basis, mounting and thermal evidence. Identical displayed watts, gas percentages or pressure cannot by themselves establish equivalent growth conditions. Use one staff-approved executable recipe; do not enter the paper's favorable ranges as though they define a tested Lesker recipe. A required seed layer, different excitation mode or added anneal changes the scope and must be recorded and agreed before the run. No public evidence inspected establishes access to the original AJA tool or recipe.

Material, temperature and dimensional tolerances must be agreed before execution. If the provider can report only an estimated substrate temperature, agree how that estimate is supported and record it as an estimate; heater setpoint or absence of a heater does not establish actual temperature. A verified below-200°C claim must not be made without adequate evidence.

**Coupon roles and processing order.**

| Coupon | Planned role | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| A | Primary: AlN thickness and texture characterization, then Al coating, then TDTR | Keep measurements on A wherever practical to preserve the strongest specimen link |
| B | Same-deposition uncoated companion and reserve | May support metrology if A is incompatible with a method. Label B's results as companion measurements; do not silently assign them to A |
| C, if included | Additional same-deposition reserve | No automatic extra metrology or TDTR. Omit if two coupons satisfy the agreed workflow |

All coupons should share the same deposition if the carrier permits; record their positions. Coupons from one deposition are not independent deposition repeats. If the provider must coat them in separate runs, identify and quote those runs separately before proceeding. An uncoated reserve is not a thermal replicate, and three TDTR spots are spatial measurements of one specimen.

1. Complete the applicable access and in-person tool qualification before hands-on operation. Obtain deposition and measurement-owner acceptance of the stack and coupon format before fabrication. Agree which steps the user operates, where qualified in-person support is required, who prepares/measures Al, and who owns analysis and file delivery. Settle handoffs and dates before the run.
2. Assign coupon IDs, photograph/inspect substrates, document oxide provenance and available thickness evidence, and record the agreed cleaning and mounting procedure. Use one parent run ID for the planned deposition.
3. The qualified user or named qualified operator performs the agreed AlN run, with the first-run support arranged with the trainer. Retain recipe, timing, tool identity, coupon-position map, observed deviations and available temperature evidence. Record which parameters are actual logged measurements and which are setpoints or operator notes.
4. Inspect the resulting film and perform thickness/texture measurements before Al coating. Prefer A; if a measurement uses B, preserve that distinction. Poor alignment or low expected conductivity alone is not a stop condition: a physically measurable underperforming film remains a valid experimental outcome.
5. Coat A with the TDTR provider's accepted Al transducer and record its actual preparation and thickness evidence. Retain B and, if present, C uncoated. Obtain agreement before any treatment that changes the AlN or interface.
6. Measure the agreed locations on A, retain raw traces and fit outputs, and assess whether conductivity and interface effects are adequately resolved. Close the cycle with a result or a documented failure/inconclusive status; do not automatically add optimization runs.

**Acceptance questions for the TDTR provider.**

- Can you accept this exact Al/AlN/SiO₂/Si stack, coupon dimensions and expected surface condition? Should you prepare the Al transducer yourselves?
- Across the plausible conductivity range, is your chosen configuration sensitive enough to AlN cross-plane conductivity? Can film conductivity and AlN/SiO₂ interface conductance be fitted separately, or will one require an independently justified constraint? Supply a brief sensitivity/identifiability rationale for the proposed model, including treatment of the Al/AlN interface and oxide layer.
- Which thicknesses, heat capacities, conductivities and interface parameters will be measured, supplied, assumed or fitted? What calibration information already exists, and does anything require a separately quoted reference sample?
- What uncertainty will be reported, what does its notation mean, and which input uncertainties and parameter correlations are included? Are the three locations and their separate results included in the base quote?
- If the signal supports only a combined thermal resistance or an assumption-dependent conductivity, how will that limitation be reported? What additional measurement, if any, would you recommend and why?
- Will the quoted deliverables include the raw traces, measurement metadata, model inputs, fit outputs and uncertainty explanation specified below?

One physical coupon is a conditional starting point, not a guarantee that every parameter is identifiable. The paper analyzes sensitivity for 200 nm AlN and shows lower conductivity sensitivity as conductivity rises; it also includes independently determined input properties in its uncertainty treatment. [SI S5 and Fig. S5, physical PDF pp. 14–15](https://poplab.stanford.edu/pdfs/Vaziri-AlNthermalMaterial3DICs-afm25.pdf). The TDTR tutorial explains why thin-film sensitivity and transducer-input uncertainties require attention and describes additional measurement configurations. That supports asking for a provider-specific plan; it does not make additional frequencies, thicknesses or reference specimens universally mandatory. [Jiang, Qian and Yang, §II.C and §IV.B](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.01258).

For anything unavailable, name the missing field or file and explain the consequence. Specify the included allowance for repeat acquisitions and routine troubleshooting in the quote. Reanalysis or another scan of the same coupon is not automatically another deposition cycle, but extra work still needs an agreed scope. A physical or measurement failure must not trigger open-ended optimization without a revised quote.

**How the cycle is counted and closed.**

- **Valid measured cycle:** the documented run produced a specimen yielding an interpretable thermal result under stated assumptions and uncertainty. Low conductivity is a valid result. A pre-agreed performance target, if later selected, is assessed separately.
- **Documented fabrication/execution failure:** the planned run was attempted, but a recorded process or sample problem prevented the agreed specimen or measurement. Deliver settings, deviations, surviving logs/measurements, photographs where useful, and the point at which work stopped. This is one attempted cycle, not a low-conductivity data label.
- **Documented measurement failure:** fabrication occurred, but a specific acquisition/preparation problem prevented a valid thermal result. Preserve the observed data and diagnosis; do not assign a material-performance value.
- **Inconclusive cycle:** usable signals were obtained, but film conductivity cannot be constrained adequately, uncertainty prevents the intended decision, or essential inputs/linkage are missing. Report the limitation and any defensible alternative observable without relabeling it as intrinsic AlN conductivity.
- **Not executed:** a preflight gate prevented deposition. This is feasibility/quoting work, not an experimental cycle.

There is no default requirement for TEM, XPS, in-plane suspended-membrane measurements, electrical testing, a thickness series or independent deposition repeats in this scope. Those may become separate work only when tied to a new question or a documented obstacle to the agreed measurement.
