# Taiwan backup: paid AlN thin-film services

Research checked October 4, 2026. California is the selected first-cycle location; this is an alternate future route, not a recommendation to move the work. No outreach, bookings, or purchases were made.

## Best backup inquiry: TCFST semiconductor center, Hsinchu

**Verified service offering:** The official 自強工業科學基金會 semiconductor-center page explicitly lists AlN coating outsourcing (氮化鋁鍍膜代工), aluminum coating, low-temperature sputter coating, magnetic sputtering, ellipsometry, and α-STEP profilometry. It directs outsourcing inquiries to **Mr. Chang / 張先生**, **yhchang@tcfst.org.tw**, **03-5722644 / 03-5733477**, at NTHU Engineering Building III, room 302, 101 Guangfu Road Section 2, Hsinchu. Listed office hours are weekdays 08:00–17:00. This is a staff-service inquiry route, rather than a request to a professor to collaborate. [Official service page](https://semi.tcfst.org.tw/Semi/Semiconductor.asp).

**Not yet verified:** acceptance of an unaffiliated individual; a one-run minimum; acceptance of 2–3 diced Si(100)/approximately 14 nm SiO₂ coupons; reactive **DC** magnetron sputtering rather than RF; approximately 200 nm AlN; actual substrate temperature below 200°C; retention/export of actual recipe logs; XRD alignment measurements; transducer quality; price; turnaround. Its listed low-temperature sputtering and AlN services are not proof that those capabilities are available together under this exact specification. The separate RF-sputter page returned a text-decoding error, so no unseen specifications have been attributed to it.

**Narrow inquiry to make later if needed:** Can the center quote one staff-operated deposition with the exact stack/temperature/process mode, measured thickness, and return of raw process records, and accept payment in an individual's name? Ask separately about an Al transducer only after the TDTR provider specifies its requirements.

## Secondary commercial lead: UVAT / 友威科技

UVAT's own LED-application page explicitly offers **AlN coating outsourcing** and lists d/n/k, α-STEP, XRD, AFM, spectroscopy, and stress-measurement capability. This confirms a genuine AlN-film service lead, rather than metallization of a bulk AlN ceramic. However, that same AlN section emphasizes high-temperature vacuum technology. It does not establish suitability for the required sub-200°C process, SiO₂ coupons, reactive DC mode, small orders, individual customers, fees, or turnaround. Do not borrow the sizes or low-temperature claims from the page's separate silver/metal sections as AlN specifications. [UVAT AlN service section](https://www.uvat.com/equimentled.html).

## TSRI and the unresolved thermal step

TSRI publishes an industry **staff-operated** processing route; industry self-operation is not offered. Published Tainan rates include SE-003 metal sputtering at **NT$2,800/hour**, SE-030 cluster RF sputtering at **NT$40/minute**, and SE-007 N&K thickness analysis at **NT$1,500/hour**; commissioned work below 30 minutes is billed as 30 minutes. These are equipment-service rates, **not an AlN quote**. I did not verify an offered AlN reactive-DC recipe or individual eligibility. [Official fee table](https://www.tsri.org.tw/tw/tech/charges_tainan.jsp). The equipment list gives SE-030 contacts **yhli@niar.org.tw / ymyeh@niar.org.tw**, extensions **6629 / 6621**, and links to the application system. [Official equipment/contact list](https://www.tsri.org.tw/tw/commonPage.jsp?kindId=F0004).

**No Taiwan one-off paid TDTR service was verified in this search.** Taiwan equipment-vendor pages discussing TDTR establish an instrument/product offering, not sample-testing acceptance. Academic TDTR publications likewise do not establish paid access. Laser-flash diffusivity services should not be substituted for TDTR on this 200 nm multilayer stack. Therefore none of the Taiwan leads above is a confirmed end-to-end route. The exact deposition match and a contracted TDTR measurement remain quote-stage questions.
