## A A detail already visible in the main paper

Open [PDF page 3](https://poplab.stanford.edu/pdfs/Vaziri-AlNthermalMaterial3DICs-afm25.pdf#page=3). Look at the stack inset inside the top-left graph, Figure 2a.

Candidate reading: the top layer is labeled **Al, 80 nm**. Therefore Q11 is already answered by the main paper, even though the plain-text extraction misses the label.

Check: is that label present, and have we assigned it to the right source?

## B A warm substrate without active heating

Open [PDF page 10](https://poplab.stanford.edu/pdfs/Vaziri-AlNthermalMaterial3DICs-afm25.pdf#page=10). Read the first paragraph beneath **S1. AlN sputter deposition**, near the bottom of the page.

Candidate reading: the authors describe no active substrate heating/cooling, while substrate temperature stabilizes **mostly between 90 and 130 °C**.

Check: does this support keeping control mode and observed temperature as separate fields? Preserve “mostly”; do not turn the range into a setpoint, a universal bound or a temperature trace for every run.

## C Measurement positions are not deposition repetitions

Open [PDF page 11](https://poplab.stanford.edu/pdfs/Vaziri-AlNthermalMaterial3DICs-afm25.pdf#page=11). Read the last three sentences of the second paragraph, immediately before **S2. X-ray diffraction analysis**.

Candidate reading: **three points on each sample** were measured by TDTR, and their variation contributes to the error treatment.

Check: does the passage describe positions within a sample, rather than three independent deposition runs? This checks Q13's interpretation. It does not by itself prove the broader Q14 absence claim.
